to be = what you are like

I am (I’m) · You / We / They are (’re) · He / She / It is (’s)

12 years old · Portuguese · tall · funny

Negative: I’m not · isn’t · aren’t

have got = what you have

I / You / We / They have got (’ve got) · He / She / It has got (’s got)

a brother · a cat · short hair · blue eyes

Negative: haven’t got · hasn’t got
